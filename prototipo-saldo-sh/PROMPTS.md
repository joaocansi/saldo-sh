## 1. Exploração da apresentação

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: polished original brand identity concept sheet for a developer-oriented local-first personal finance project.
Primary request: Create the proposed brand "saldo.sh". The name is spelled exactly s a l d o . s h, entirely lowercase. It should feel like a well-crafted independent open-source developer tool, not a consumer bank, cryptocurrency exchange or luxury fintech.
Symbol: A minimal flat amber COIN, a circular solid disc with a very small second crescent rim offset to the lower left suggesting a stack of money. Cut a crisp dark terminal prompt ">_" into the front coin: one right-pointing chevron followed by one underscore, optically centered, thick strokes, recognizable at small size. The rim must be an abstract flat solid shape, not a realistic metallic edge. This is a money-plus-code logo, simple silhouette, no extra symbols.
Wordmark: "saldo.sh" in a confident medium-heavy modern monospaced lowercase typeface, thoughtfully kerned; "saldo" off-white and ".sh" amber on dark. No rounded n-shaped monograms, no bubble letters.
Composition: a flat landscape identity sheet with generous editorial whitespace, carefully aligned technical grid. Large upper area on uniform solid graphite #171B20, showing amber symbol to the left of a large single-line wordmark. Small supporting copy below, exact "Seu dinheiro. Suas regras." in muted off-white. Small top caption "FINANÇAS PESSOAIS / LOCAL-FIRST". Lower strip contains three equal-width neatly divided panels: first a light-background variant of the same full logo on solid #F3F1E9 with graphite lettering and amber symbol; second a square graphite app icon with the same amber coin symbol on a uniform muted sage background #ADBBAE; third pure white monochrome coin symbol with the same white wordmark on solid graphite, and three small palette swatches at the bottom. All logo instances must have the identical coin and terminal mark. Restrict writing to the above wordmark, caption, tagline and tiny exact palette values "#171B20", "#E9B45C", "#F3F1E9".
Color palette: graphite #171B20, amber #E9B45C, off-white #F3F1E9, muted sage #ADBBAE.
Style/medium: crisp flat 2D graphic design with SVG-like sharp edges, disciplined and understated, professional finished brand study.
Constraints: Absolutely UNIFORM SOLID backgrounds and shapes. NO shadows, NO gradients, NO haze, NO lighting, NO blur, NO photography, NO texture, NO glow, NO bevels, NO embossed mockups, NO 3D. No dollar signs, no crypto symbols, no bank cards, no leaves, no Nubank likeness, no mascots. Do not invent GitHub stars, badges, licenses, domains, endorsements or UI features. No watermarks.
